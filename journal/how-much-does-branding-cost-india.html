How much does branding cost in India?

By SankalpThe Creative Audit

If you've started Googling this, you've found answers ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹50 lakh — which is useless. Here's why the number is so wide, and how to control which end you land on.

The honest answer: branding costs whatever the ambition behind it costs. It isn't a product with a sticker price; it's a function of scope, the stage of your business, and how much thinking has to happen before the making. Still, you deserve something concrete, so here's a rough map.

A rough map of the market

A freelance logo and a basic identity typically runs ₹40,000 to ₹1.5 lakh. A studio doing a full identity — mark, system, guidelines, applications — sits around ₹2 to 8 lakh and up. A complete brand system with strategy, naming, packaging, and web climbs well into double-digit lakhs. These numbers move with the experience of the people doing the work and the depth of thinking involved — not the size of the final file.

Why the range is so wide

Most of the cost isn't the design. It's the uncertainty around it. A clear, specific brief lets a designer move fast and price tightly. A vague one forces them to guess, iterate, and pad — and you pay for every wrong turn, every "can we see another direction," every month the project drifts.

The most expensive thing in any branding project is not the design. It's the lack of clarity around it.

How to actually control the cost

If you want to control what branding costs, control the brief. Decide what your brand is supposed to do — who it's for, what's changing, what success looks like — before anyone opens a design tool. Get that right and you stop paying for revisions that are really just the two of you discovering the goal in real time. Clarity is the cheapest line item in the whole project, and it's the one that decides whether the expensive lines were worth it.

The audit exists to make that one decision — what to build, and why — before you spend on the expensive part.
